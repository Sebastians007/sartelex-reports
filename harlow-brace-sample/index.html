Part 2

The 5 fixes worth doing first

  1. Put one button on the home page that says Get a quote for your siteIt turns a reader into a named enquiry in one click instead of sending them hunting. Roughly an afternoon.
  2. Cut the enquiry form to name, site and phone numberFloor area and budget are questions for the call, not for a stranger deciding whether to bother. Roughly an afternoon.
  3. Add two named jobs with a quote from the person who signed them offA care home manager believes another care home manager. Logos alone say nothing. Roughly a week.
  4. Answer the term time question on the services pageIt is the first thing a school asks and right now they have to ring to find out. Roughly an afternoon.
  5. Break the services page into five headed sectionsNobody reads nine hundred unbroken words. They skim, find nothing, and leave. Roughly a day.
